LCEdit: a fast, free clip cutter for Windows

LCEdit, also called the LC Editor, trims and joins your game clips without opening a full video editor. Mark the parts you want, press Cut & Save, and the new clip is ready in seconds.

It comes built into LCClips: select a clip and press ✂ Edit in LC Editor, or open it from File → Open LC Editor.

About LCClips

Features

  • Mark In and Out with the I and O keys, or drag the handles on the timeline.
  • Join several parts: press A to add another section. Parts are joined in order into one clip.
  • Fast mode: a lossless copy that's near-instant, with no quality loss. The start snaps to the nearest keyframe.
  • Precise mode: frame-exact cuts, re-encoded on your GPU.
  • Back in your library right away: cuts saved to your clips folder show up in LCClips immediately.
  • A keyboard-shortcut list is one click away in the editor's header.

FAQ

Can I download LCEdit on its own?

Not right now. LCEdit ships inside LCClips, so installing LCClips gives you both.

Does cutting a clip lower its quality?

Not in Fast mode, which copies the video without re-encoding. Precise mode re-encodes on your GPU so the cut lands on the exact frame.

Is it free?

Yes. LCEdit and LCClips are free and open source under the MIT License, with no watermark and no account.
